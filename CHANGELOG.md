# Changelog

All notable changes to **Bounce Zone** (formerly Ball Bouncer Clicker).

## [2.0.0] - Complete Rethink: Bounce Zone

### Changed
- 🔄 **Complete game redesign** — Replaced clicker mechanic with neon survival dodge arena
- 🎮 **New controls** — Drag/arrow-key movement replaces tapping
- ⚡ **Reflex-based gameplay** — Dodge hazards, survive waves, high-skill ceiling

### Added
- 🏀 **Player ball** — Fast responsive movement with wall bouncing
- ⚠️ **6 hazard types** — Spinner blades, chasers, mines, bursts, orbit orbs, and combinations
- 🌊 **Wave system** — 10+ escalating waves with increasing difficulty
- 🏆 **Survival scoring** — Score gems, survival bonus, wave completion bonuses
- ✨ **Power-ups** — Score Magnet, Invincibility, Slow Time, Shield, Extra Life
- 🎨 **Full neon visual overhaul** — Glowing particles, screen shake, dark theme
- 📖 **How-to-play screen** — Clear tutorial describing controls and hazards
- 🎵 **Grace periods** — 3s invincibility at each wave start

### Removed
- ❌ Tap-to-earn clicker mechanic
- ❌ Brick-breaker levels and paddle
- ❌ Old power-up & achievement systems (replaced with new ones)

- Tap-to-earn idle clicker gameplay
- Score multipliers every 100 taps
- Auto-save progress with SharedPreferences
- Neon particle effects on tap
